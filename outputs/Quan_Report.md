# Quantitative Data Report

## Key Business Observations and Trends

### 1. Declining Upgrade Conversion Rate
The conversion rate from the Free tier to the Pro tier has steadily declined from 1.8% in January 2026 to 1.2% in April 2026. This indicates a growing friction or lack of perceived value in the upgrade path.

### 2. Increasing Pro User Churn Rate
The churn rate for Pro tier users has increased from 4.2% in January 2026 to 5.5% in April 2026. While the number of Pro users grew slightly early in the quarter, it declined from March (19,200) to April (19,000).

### 3. Slowing Revenue Growth Despite Increasing MAU
Overall Monthly Active Users (MAU) continue to grow (from 140,000 in Jan to 150,000 in Apr), driven mostly by Free users. However, Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) growth is decelerating due to the compounding effect of declining conversions and rising Pro churn.
