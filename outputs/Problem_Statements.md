# Prioritized Problem Statements

**North Star Metric**: Active Paid Subscribers (Pro & Elite)

### 1. The Upgrade Confusion Problem
**Problem**: Free tier users are abandoning the upgrade flow at an 88% rate because the value proposition of the Pro tier (specifically "Advanced Analytics") is vague and tooltips explaining these features are broken on mobile.
**Impact on North Star**: This directly causes the declining Free-to-Pro upgrade conversion rate (from 1.8% to 1.2%), preventing the acquisition of new Active Paid Subscribers, which is a key Q3 strategic goal.

### 2. The Dangerous Meal Planner Problem
**Problem**: The meal planner is ignoring user-configured dietary restrictions and allergies, suggesting unsafe meals, which leads to immediate app abandonment and trust erosion.
**Impact on North Star**: This severe bug destroys product value for health-conscious users, driving up the Pro User Churn Rate (increased from 4.2% to 5.5%) and preventing users from retaining their paid subscriptions.

### 3. The Broken Checkout Experience Problem
**Problem**: Users who attempt to purchase a subscription face a 50% drop-off at the checkout form due to a poorly formatted zip code error message that is unreadable on mobile screens.
**Impact on North Star**: This friction prevents highly-intent users from successfully completing their purchase, directly bottlenecking the growth of Active Paid Subscribers and MRR.

### 4. The Missing Trainer Notifications Problem
**Problem**: Elite users and their trainers are missing scheduled check-ins because push notifications for in-app messages are failing to deliver.
**Impact on North Star**: This degrades the core value proposition of the $49.99/mo Elite tier (1-on-1 coaching), which threatens the Q3 strategic goal of reducing Elite tier churn by 10%.
