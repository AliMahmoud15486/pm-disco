# Testable Hypotheses

### Hypothesis 1: Clear Value Proposition (Addresses Upgrade Confusion)
**If** we redesign the Pro tier upgrade page to replace the vague "Advanced Analytics" text with concrete visual examples of the reports users will receive, and fix the broken tooltips on mobile Safari...
**Then** the `view_upgrade_page` to `click_subscribe_button` conversion rate will increase by 20%, and the overall Free-to-Pro upgrade conversion rate will stabilize above 1.5%.
**Because** users will clearly understand what they are paying for, reducing hesitation and friction.

### Hypothesis 2: Safe Meal Planning (Addresses Dangerous Meal Planner)
**If** we fix the backend logic of the meal planner to strictly filter out ingredients based on the user's saved dietary restrictions and allergies...
**Then** the Pro tier churn rate will decrease by 1%, and we will see a reduction in app abandonment immediately following the "Refresh Suggestions" action.
**Because** users will regain trust in the app's core nutritional guidance and find value in the personalized recommendations.

### Hypothesis 3: Frictionless Checkout (Addresses Broken Checkout Experience)
**If** we fix the mobile CSS to ensure error messages on the checkout form (specifically the zip code field) are fully visible, and implement inline validation...
**Then** the `view_checkout_form` to `submit_payment` conversion rate will increase from 50% to at least 70%.
**Because** users will be able to easily identify and correct their payment entry errors without abandoning the form.

### Hypothesis 4: Reliable Notifications (Addresses Missing Trainer Notifications)
**If** we investigate and resolve the push notification delivery failures for in-app messages between Elite users and trainers...
**Then** the Elite tier churn rate will decrease towards our Q3 goal of a 10% reduction.
**Because** users will reliably receive their coaching, maintaining the perceived value of the premium service.
