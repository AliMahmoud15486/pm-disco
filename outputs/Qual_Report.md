# Qualitative Research Report

## Top 3 User Problems

### 1. Meal Planner Ignores Dietary Restrictions
Users report that the meal planner suggests meals containing ingredients they have explicitly marked as allergies or dietary restrictions in their settings.
- **Evidence**:
  - App Review: "I marked a severe gluten allergy and the very first 'suggested meal' was a whole wheat pasta dish." (User: FitnessFanatic99)
  - Interview (Sarah M.): "It keeps recommending meals with dairy, and I've set my profile to dairy-free three times now."
  - Support Ticket (T-1044): "Meal planner suggesting recipes with peanuts even though I marked peanut allergy in settings. Very dangerous."

### 2. Ambiguous Pro Tier Value Proposition
Free users are confused about what features are actually included in the Pro tier, specifically what "Advanced Analytics" entails, making them hesitant to upgrade.
- **Evidence**:
  - App Review: "Wanted to upgrade to Pro because I need more data, but the pricing page doesn't explain what 'Advanced Analytics' means." (User: RunningMan)
  - Interview (Sarah M.): "I couldn't tell what I was actually getting for $9.99. The feature list was super vague."
  - Support Ticket (T-1048): "The upgrade screen is really confusing. It says 'Advanced Analytics' but what does that actually track?"

### 3. Missing Push Notifications for Elite Users
Elite tier users and trainers are experiencing issues where push notifications for in-app messages or scheduled check-ins fail to deliver.
- **Evidence**:
  - App Review: "Only issue is sometimes I don't get notifications when he messages me." (User: LiftHeavy)
  - Support Ticket (T-1046): "My trainer sent me a message but I didn't get a push notification. I missed our scheduled check-in."
