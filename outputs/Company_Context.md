# Company Context: FitTrack Pro

## Business Overview
- **Industry**: Health & Fitness SaaS
- **Product**: Mobile app and web dashboard for tracking workouts, nutrition, and connecting with personal trainers.
- **Business Model**: Freemium model with three tiers: Free, Pro ($9.99/mo), and Elite ($49.99/mo).
- **Target Audience**: Fitness Enthusiasts, Beginners, and Personal Trainers.

## Strategic Goals for Q3 2026
1. Increase conversion rate from Free to Pro tier by 15%.
2. Reduce churn rate of Elite tier users by 10%.
3. Expand the personal trainer network by onboarding 500 new coaches.

## North Star Metric
**Active Paid Subscribers (Pro & Elite)**
*Rationale:* The primary strategic goals for Q3 2026 focus on increasing Pro conversion and retaining Elite users. Therefore, growing the total number of Active Paid Subscribers directly reflects the business's success in delivering value that users are willing to pay for, aligning with the freemium revenue model.
