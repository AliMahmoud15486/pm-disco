Company Name: FitTrack Pro
Industry: Health & Fitness SaaS
Product: A mobile app and web dashboard for tracking workouts, nutrition, and connecting with personal trainers.

Business Model:
Freemium model. 
- Free Tier: Basic workout tracking and community forum access.
- Pro Tier ($9.99/mo): Advanced analytics, custom meal plans, and ad-free experience.
- Elite Tier ($49.99/mo): 1-on-1 coaching with certified personal trainers via in-app messaging and video calls.

Target Audience:
1. Fitness Enthusiasts: People who work out 3+ times a week and want detailed analytics.
2. Beginners: People looking for guidance and structured plans.
3. Personal Trainers: Professionals looking to manage their clients and find new leads.

Strategic Goals for Q3 2026:
- Increase conversion rate from Free to Pro tier by 15%.
- Reduce churn rate of Elite tier users by 10%.
- Expand the personal trainer network by onboarding 500 new coaches.
