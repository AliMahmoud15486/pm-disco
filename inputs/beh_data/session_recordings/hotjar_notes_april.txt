Session Recording Analysis - April 2026
Tool: Hotjar
Total Sessions Reviewed: 50

Observation 1: The Upgrade Page (URL: /upgrade)
- Out of 20 users who landed on the upgrade page, 18 spent an average of 45 seconds scrolling up and down the feature comparison table.
- We observed "rage clicking" on the tooltip icon next to "Advanced Analytics" in the Pro tier column. The tooltip appears to be broken on mobile Safari; it doesn't open when tapped.
- Users frequently scrolled to the bottom, scrolled back to the top, and then abandoned the page without clicking "Subscribe".

Observation 2: The Checkout Form (URL: /checkout)
- 10 users reached the checkout form. 5 successfully completed the purchase.
- The other 5 users abandoned the form after entering their credit card details.
- In 3 of these cases, the user attempted to submit the form, but a red error message appeared near the zip code field. The users tried re-entering the zip code twice before closing the tab. The error message was not fully visible on mobile screens.

Observation 3: Meal Planner (URL: /dashboard/meals)
- Users frequently navigate to Settings > Profile > Dietary Restrictions, check a box (e.g., "Dairy-Free"), and immediately navigate to the Meal Planner.
- We observed users clicking "Refresh Suggestions" multiple times.
- Several users highlighted the ingredients list of the suggested meal with their cursor, lingered for a few seconds, and then closed the app entirely.
